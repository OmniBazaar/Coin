# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

We take the security of OmniCoin seriously. If you find a vulnerability in the
smart contracts in this repository, please report it privately.

**Do not open a public issue.** Public disclosure of a live vulnerability can
put user funds at risk.

**Report to:** `security@omnibazaar.com`

Please include:

- A short description of the issue.
- The affected contract and function.
- Steps to reproduce the issue, or a proof of concept.
- The impact (what an attacker could do).

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within **72 hours**.
- We respond with a full assessment within **14 days**.
- We credit researchers who report valid vulnerabilities, unless you ask us
  not to.
- We do not pay bounties at this time.

## Scope

This policy covers the smart contracts in this repository, including the
deployed contracts listed in `deployments/mainnet.json`.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action against anyone who, in good faith:

- Reports a vulnerability to us before disclosing it publicly.
- Does not access, modify, or delete user funds.
- Does not disrupt the network.

Please give us a reasonable amount of time to fix a vulnerability before
disclosing it publicly.
